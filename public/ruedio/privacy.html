Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ruedio ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the Ruedio mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Phone number — used for account creation and SMS verification (one-time passcode)
  • Email address — optionally used for account login
  • Name and profile details — provided during registration
  • Vehicle information — provided by you for service requests
  • Location data — used to match you with nearby service providers
  • Payment information — processed securely via Stripe; we do not store card numbers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To send one-time passcodes (OTP) for phone number verification
  • To connect customers with service providers
  • To process payments for services
  • To send service-related notifications
  • To improve our platform and prevent fraud

3. SMS and Phone Communications

By providing your phone number and requesting a verification code, you consent to receive a one-time SMS verification code from Ruedio. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message. Reply HELP for assistance.

We use Twilio to deliver SMS messages. Your phone number is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers necessary to operate our platform (Twilio, Stripe, Firebase)
  • When required by law or legal process
  • Between customers and providers to fulfill a service booking (name, vehicle, location)

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), hashed tokens, and access controls to protect your data. OTP codes expire within 10 minutes and are never stored after verification.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@savateck.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
