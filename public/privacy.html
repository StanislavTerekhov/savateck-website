This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how SAVATECK, a California corporation (formerly PrimeFix Solution Corp), together with its subsidiaries and affiliates ("SAVATECK", "Company", "we", "our", or "us"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects information about you when you access or use our website at savateck.com and our products, including Ruedio (a technology platform that connects customers with independent third-party service providers) and Ruedio Task (the provider-facing application used by those independent providers), together with all related applications, websites, features, and services (collectively, the "Services").

SAVATECK operates the Services as a neutral technology marketplace and venue. We do not perform automotive, repair, detailing, roadside, or other physical services ourselves; those services are performed by independent third-party providers who are not employees or agents of SAVATECK. This Policy describes our data practices only. The conduct of independent providers and customers is governed by our Terms of Service.

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, do not access or use the Services. This Policy is incorporated into and forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Scope & Who This Applies To

This Policy applies to "Customers" (users who request services through Ruedio), "Providers" (independent contractors who offer services through Ruedio Task), visitors to our website, and other individuals who interact with us. The Services are intended for users located in the United States, and the Services are designed to comply with U.S. federal law and the laws of the State of California.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account & identity: name, email address, mobile phone number, password (for email sign-up), and authentication identifiers when you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Profile: profile photo, vehicle information (make, model, year, color, license plate), and, for Providers, business name, years of experience, services offered, pricing, and service cities.
  • Booking & location: service address, the city/service area you select, geolocation when you enable it, scheduling, notes, and order history.
  • Provider verification: identity and licensing documents, insurance documents, government-issued ID, selfies, business filings, and information used for background or eligibility checks.
  • Communications: in-app chat messages between Customers and Providers, support requests, reviews and ratings, and your correspondence with us.
  • Payment & payout: we use third-party payment processors (including Stripe) to process payments and provider payouts. Card numbers and bank details are collected and stored by those processors, not by us; we receive limited information such as the last four digits, card brand, payment status, and payout status.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & usage: IP address, device and browser type, operating system, app version, identifiers, pages and screens viewed, actions taken, and timestamps.
  • Location: approximate location derived from IP, and, with your permission, precise device location used for matching, dispatch, and live tracking.
  • Push notifications: if you enable notifications, we collect a device push token (via the Apple Push Notification service and our notification provider) to send booking, job, and account alerts. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: used to keep you signed in, remember preferences, secure the Services, and measure performance.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Identity providers (Google, Apple) when you choose to sign in with them — typically your name, email, and a unique identifier.
  • Payment processors (Stripe) regarding transaction and payout status.
  • Communications providers (e.g., Twilio for SMS verification, email delivery vendors) regarding message delivery.
  • Verification or background-check vendors, where applicable, regarding Provider eligibility.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Services and create and manage accounts.
  • Match Customers with available Providers by service and city, dispatch requests, and enable live tracking and communication.
  • Process payments, authorize and capture charges, calculate and remit Provider payouts, and handle refunds, holds, and chargebacks through our processors.
  • Verify identity and eligibility, including Provider verification and fraud prevention.
  • Send one-time passcodes (OTP) for sign-in and phone verification, and transactional notifications about your bookings.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • Monitor, analyze, and improve the Services, develop new features, and ensure quality and safety.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, unsafe, or illegal activity, and enforce our Terms.
  • Comply with legal obligations and exercise or defend legal claims.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only as described below:

  • Between Customers and Providers: to facilitate a booking, we share the limited information necessary to complete the service — for example, the Customer's first name, service address, vehicle details, and a contact phone number with the assigned Provider, and the Provider's name and profile with the Customer. You acknowledge that once you participate in a booking, the other party may retain certain information you share with them, and SAVATECK does not control their use of it.
  • Service providers & processors who perform functions on our behalf, including payment processing (Stripe), authentication and database hosting (Supabase), SMS and one-time-passcode delivery (Twilio), email delivery (e.g., Resend), cloud infrastructure and notifications (Google Cloud / Firebase), and analytics. These parties are authorized to use personal information only as necessary to provide services to us.
  • Legal, safety, and protection: to comply with law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms; to protect the rights, property, safety, or security of SAVATECK, our users, or the public; and to detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or bankruptcy, information may be transferred as a business asset.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended ("CCPA/CPRA").

We do not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.
